Butter Chicken

Description

Butter chicken is a very popular Indian dish made with tender chicken cooked in a rich, creamy tomato-based sauce with aromatic spices.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Butter
  • Tomatoes
  • Cream
  • Curd
  • Ginger
  • Garlic
  • Garam Masala
  • Turmeric
  • Red chilli powder
  • Salt
  • Fresh coriander

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken with curd, ginger, garlic, and spices.
  2. Cook the marinated chicken until it is tender and lightly browned.
  3. Prepare the tomato-based sauce with butter, ginger, garlic, and spices.
  4. Add the cooked chicken to the sauce and simmer for several minutes.
  5. Add the cream, stir well and cook until the sauce becomes rich and creamy.
  6. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.
