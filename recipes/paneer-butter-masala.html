Paneer Butter Masala

Description

Paneer Butter Masala is a popular North Indian dish made with soft paneer cooked in a rich and creamy tomato-based gravy with aromatic spices.

Ingredients

  • Paneer
  • Butter
  • Tomatoes
  • Cream
  • Curd
  • Ginger
  • Garlic
  • Garam masala
  • Turmeric
  • Red chilli powder
  • Salt
  • Fresh coriander

Steps

  1. Cut the paneer into cubes and lightly fry them in butter.
  2. Prepare the tomato-based gravy with butter, ginger, garlic, and spices.
  3. Add the cooked paneer to the gravy and simmer for several minutes.
  4. Add the cream, stir well, and cook until the gravy becomes rich and creamy.
  5. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.
