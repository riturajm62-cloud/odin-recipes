Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a delicious Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich tomato sauce,cheese, and a savory fillings.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F(190°C).
  2. Cook the ground beef with onion and garlic until browned.
  3. Add tomato sauce and Italian seasoning, then simmer for a few minutes
  4. Layer the noodles, meat sauce, and cheeses in a baking dish.
  5. Repeat the layers and finish with mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  6. Bake until the cheese is melted and golden.
  7. Let the lasagna cool for a few minutes before serving.
